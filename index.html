About

Las Vegas Punx is the ultimate digital hub for the Las Vegas punk rock scene. Whether you're a local looking for DIY basement shows or a tourist hunting for punk rock concerts in Las Vegas, our event calendar has you covered.

We track everything from legendary acts hitting the Backstage Bar and House of Blues to underground gigs at The Double Down and The Dive Bar. Our mission is to connect fans with local Vegas bands, provide the latest punk music news, and make sure you never miss a beat in the 702.

Check our weekly show listings to see who's playing tonight, or submit your own show to get the word out to the community.
