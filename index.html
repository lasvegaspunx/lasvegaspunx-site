About

Run by real people who actually go to the shows. Not a corporate events app that added a "punk" filter.

We track it all: legendary bands at Backstage Bar and House of Blues, underground gigs at The Double Down and The Dive Bar, DIY shows at spots you've never heard of. If it's happening, we've got it.

Local or just blowing through, check out our calendar and see who's playing tonight. Got a show? Submit it, we'll help spread the word.

For the opinions, interviews, and everything that doesn't fit on a flyer, head to Sucker Punk to check out our zine.
